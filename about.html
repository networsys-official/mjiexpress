About Company

A Logistics Partner You Can Count On

MJI Xpress Global is a full-service logistics, freight and courier company. We began with a small fleet and a big promise: deliver every shipment safely, on time and at a fair price.

Today our network spans more than 200 countries, backed by modern technology, real-time tracking and a passionate team that treats every parcel like its own.

  • Door-to-door global delivery
  • Transparent, money-saving pricing
  • Live tracking and 24/7 support
  • Certified customs specialists
